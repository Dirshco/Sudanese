2. Purpose of the Gathering

Beyond a social event: connect, listen, organize, and plan.

The GLSA Men’s Summer Gathering is designed to provide a welcoming, informal environment where members of the Sudanese community can:

  • Connect, socialize, and strengthen relationships.
  • Learn about GLSA’s activities and accomplishments during the past year.
  • Discuss the community’s priorities and future goals.
  • Share ideas, concerns, and constructive feedback.
  • Identify opportunities for greater community participation and volunteerism.
  • Enjoy food, recreation, and fellowship in a community setting.

3. Teams, Leadership & Responsibilities

Each team has a clear operational role before, during, and after the event.

1

Park & Logistics

Lead: Organizing Committee

Before the Event

  • Confirm the pavilion reservation and event arrangements.
  • Verify park regulations, including charcoal grills, food preparation, waste disposal, and facility use.
  • Develop the site layout for tables, seating, food service, activities, and gathering areas.
  • Confirm availability of tables, chairs, trash receptacles, and required supplies.

Event Setup

  • Arrive by 9:00 AM for site preparation.
  • Arrange tables and seating.
  • Set up food-service and beverage areas.
  • Establish the designated grilling area.
  • Set up the sound system and microphone, if applicable.
  • Complete a safety and readiness check before guests arrive.

After the Event

  • Coordinate complete cleanup of the pavilion and surrounding area.
  • Collect and dispose of all trash appropriately.
  • Remove or return tables, chairs, equipment, and other materials as required.
  • Conduct a final inspection to ensure the park is left clean and in good condition.
2

BBQ & Culinary

Lead: Organizing Committee

Food Preparation

  • Purchase the required quantities of lamb and chicken.
  • Marinate the meat approximately 24 hours before the event.
  • Store all meat under appropriate refrigerated conditions until transportation.

Transportation

  • Transport meat to the park in properly chilled/refrigerated coolers.
  • Maintain appropriate food temperatures during transportation and preparation.
  • Bring charcoal, grilling tools, gloves, trays, foil, and other BBQ supplies.

Grilling & Food Safety

  • Set up the grill in the designated area.
  • Manage charcoal ignition and fire safety.
  • Establish a grilling schedule for efficient service.
  • Monitor food preparation and cooking temperatures.
  • Prevent cross-contamination between raw and cooked foods.
  • Keep cooked food covered and protected until serving.
  • Coordinate serving times with the Sides & Refreshments Team.
3

Appetizers, Sides & Refreshments

Lead: Organizing Committee

Food & Side Dishes

  • Prepare fresh green salad (Salata Khadra) on the morning of the event.
  • Prepare cucumber-yogurt salad and other planned side dishes.
  • Arrange pita bread, dips, chips, and other appetizers.
  • Coordinate quantities based on expected attendance.

Beverages

  • Provide sufficient bottled water.
  • Provide sodas and juices.
  • Prepare tea and coffee, if included in the final program.
  • Maintain a designated beverage station throughout the event.

Serving Supplies

  • Heavy-duty disposable plates
  • Cups
  • Forks, knives, and spoons
  • Napkins
  • Serving spoons and utensils
  • Food trays and containers
  • Aluminum foil/plastic wrap
  • Trash bags and food-storage containers

Serving & Presentation

  • Organize the food-service area before guests arrive.
  • Coordinate with the BBQ Team so food is served promptly.
  • Keep serving areas clean and organized.
  • Monitor supplies throughout the event and replenish as needed.
4

Program & Community Facilitation

Lead: Organizing Committee

GLSA Annual Update

  • Prepare a concise 15-minute presentation highlighting GLSA’s activities and accomplishments.
  • Include major initiatives, programs, community activities, achievements, and relevant statistics.
  • Prepare both digital and printed versions.

Community Discussion

  • Introduce the purpose and objectives of the gathering.
  • Facilitate an open and respectful discussion.
  • Encourage participation from members with different perspectives.
  • Keep discussion constructive and focused on community needs.

Community Feedback

  • GLSA App: Submit feedback through a designated digital form.
  • In-Person: Share comments during the discussion.
  • Phone: Contact a designated GLSA representative after the event.

Priorities & Future Goals

  • Record and organize issues and suggestions raised.
  • Identify recurring themes and community priorities.
  • Summarize proposed goals for the upcoming year.
  • Develop a post-event summary for GLSA leadership.

Membership & Dues

  • Explain the importance and purpose of GLSA membership.
  • Address common questions or concerns regarding membership.
  • Provide clear information about membership dues and their purpose.
  • Provide an easy mechanism for members to join or renew.
5

Games & Recreational Activities

Sports & Recreation

  • Other outdoor games
  • Playing cards 🃏

6. Post-Event Follow-Up

Turn the gathering into measurable community action.

  1. Review all feedback received during the event.
  2. Review submissions received through the GLSA app.
  3. Identify the most frequently mentioned community priorities.
  4. Organize suggestions into short-, medium-, and long-term goals.
  5. Prepare a summary of the gathering.
  6. Share appropriate outcomes with the broader GLSA community.
  7. Identify volunteers and members interested in specific initiatives.
  8. Establish next steps and responsible individuals or teams for agreed-upon priorities.
Proposed Outcome

The gathering should not be viewed simply as a social event. It should serve as an opportunity to listen, connect, organize, and establish a clear roadmap for the future of GLSA and the Sudanese community in Greater Lansing.
